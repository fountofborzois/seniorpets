Other Links

Thank you for taking interest in the Senior Pet Adoption Foundation. We believe that every animal deserves a loving home, so we'd like to share other adoption agencies that might interest you.

National Rescue Borzoi Foundation

The NRBF is a non-profit organization dedicated to the rescue and rehabilitation of borzois. They have fosters all around the US and have even helped borzois in Ukraine. If you're interested in this beautiful breed, the NRBF is the place to help you find your new best friend.

Collie Rescue Foundation

Interested in this famous dog with its iconic bark? The CRF can help you get your own lovable collie. They are a national organization that does its best to provide a one stop shop for all your collie information, and even has working relationships with collie rescues all across the US.

Alley Cat Allies

Looking for a new family member that's not a dog? Something more feline? The ACA's got you covered. Their mission is to advocate for the treatment of all cats around the world. They have a lot of useful information about cats on their website. Check it out!
